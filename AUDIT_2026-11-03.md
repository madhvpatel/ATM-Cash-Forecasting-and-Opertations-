# CMS2 Audit: Cash Positioning, Controls and UI (3 Nov 2026)

**Scope:** `live_portal_v2/` (the canonical portal) at commit `1a92501`. I read `masters_manager.py`, `serve.py` and the approval/sweep/lock paths of `agent.py`, and scanned `index.html` for specific patterns. I ran a read-only probe against the engine with `save_overrides` stubbed out, so no repo file was written (`git status` stayed clean). The data is synthetic. Numbers below come from that probe unless a `file:line` is given.

**Status column:** *Open* = flagged in the 27 Oct `PROJECT_REVIEW.md` and still present. *New* = not covered before, or introduced by the 29 Oct work.

---

## Executive verdict

The portal is **not safe to drive real cash movement.** There are three systemic problems:

1. **The indent engine plans more cash than the machines can hold.** It also does not check its own plans.
2. **Maker/checker separation is not enforced.** Several paths approve themselves, and the AI agent can approve or lock everything because of keyword matches.
3. **Some figures shown as audit evidence are made up.** They are random or hard-coded, but the UI and reports present them as measured history and accuracy.

Fleet snapshot from the probe: cash on hand is ₹1.08 Cr and the proposed net indent is **₹3.22 Cr**, about 3× fleet cash. Every one of the 8 ATMs gets a 2-cycle plan.

## Fix order

1. **Stop the money paths.**
   - Make the manifest include only approved figures, frozen when approved. An edit should clear the approval.
   - Make the lock reject writes.
   - Put the AI agent's approve, lock, sweep and override actions behind an explicit confirmation token. Remove keyword triggers and the units heuristic.
   - Require the approver to be someone other than the maker or override author.
   - Add authentication, bind to `127.0.0.1`, and drop `CORS *`.
2. **Use physical capacity.** Make capacity the sum over cassettes of max notes × denomination. Allocate the indent per cassette against free space. Raise a warning on overflow; never clamp silently.
3. **Rebuild the policy calculation once.**
   - Base the order-up-to level on the refill date: demand from refill to next refill, plus a safety buffer.
   - Apply events per day, only within the cycle.
   - Get z from `NormalDist`.
   - Re-simulate every candidate plan and reject any that still stock out.
   - Simulate over the cycle, not a fixed 7 days.
4. **Remove made-up evidence.** Delete `np.random` actuals and the hard-coded efficacy, history and anomalies. Show "no data" where nothing was measured.
5. **Write one test for each item above.** The biggest gaps are cash conservation in sweeps, capacity, the approval lifecycle and lock enforcement.
